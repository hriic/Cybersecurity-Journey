# CTF and Lab Notes

This directory is for lessons from legal training environments such as TryHackMe and Hack The Box.

I do not want these notes to become copied walkthroughs. The useful part is recording how I approached a lab, where I got stuck, and what I learned.

## Workflow I am practicing

1. Understand the target and scope.
2. Perform discovery and enumeration.
3. Investigate exposed services.
4. Follow the evidence instead of guessing.
5. Document what worked and what did not.
6. Record the lesson I can reuse next time.

## Simple CTF lesson

One of my early CTFs helped me connect scanning, web enumeration, directory discovery and remote-access concepts. The biggest lesson was that careful enumeration usually creates the path forward.

All targets documented here are intentionally vulnerable or explicitly authorized training systems.
